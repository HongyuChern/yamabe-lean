# v0.1.0

- Complete formalization of the singular Yamabe equation on the unit round
  three-sphere with any two distinct prescribed points. The conformal metric
  has scalar curvature six and is metrically and geodesically complete.
- Positive symmetric Green functions on smooth closed connected positive-Yamabe
  three-manifolds, with the inverse-radius term and bounds on the remainder
  through second derivatives in normal Gauss coordinates.
- Metric and geodesic completeness for solutions satisfying the stated
  cylindrical lower bound and end hypotheses.
- Linear gluing in the original metric for the specified weighted source
  space, under the stated chart, phase, and boundary conditions.
- Lean 4.33.1 and pinned Lake dependencies. The round three-sphere theorem
  passed Comparator with both the Lean and Nanoda kernels.

See the [README](README.md) for theorem statements, mathematical references,
and entry points, and the [source notices](NOTICE.md) for licensing.
